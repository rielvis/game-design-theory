# Pre-Production
Once your scope and marketing is established, you can begin documenting your design.

Deliverable for this phase is a **_Game Design Document_**, composed of the following:
- Mechanics Documentation
- Interactive Documentation
- Narrative Documentation
- Aesthetic Documentation
- Level Documentation

Throughout your designs, consider the following:
- Is the game appealing? _(will it attract players)_
  - Is it charming?
  - Is it fantastical?
  - Is it comprehensible?
- Is the game fun? _(will it sustain players)_
- Is the game within scope? _(can you achieve it)_

## STEP 1 - Game Idea & Design Pillars
Every game starts with an idea or a premise. The idea could be a game mechanic, a story theme, an artistic style, or it could be as simple as a feeling or experience you want the player to have regardless of the specific game mechanic or plot line.

You can try asking yourself ‘what if’ questions, and your pursuit of the creation of this game is your attempt to answer that question.

**_To establish the initial idea for your game, create a one-line pitch that encapsulates the core of what your game is about mechanically, narratively, and aesthetically._**
