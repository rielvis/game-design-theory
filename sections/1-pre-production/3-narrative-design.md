# P1-STEP 3 : Narrative Design
The narrative design of your GDD outlines the story your game is telling. This step will first overview concepts to keep in mind when designing your story, and then it will explore some techniques for actually building the story.

Deliverables for this step are:
- _Thematic Profile_
- _Location Profiles_
- _Character Profiles_
- _Story Arc Outlines_
- _Narrative Screenplay_
