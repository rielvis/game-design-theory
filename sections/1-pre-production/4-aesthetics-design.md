# P1-STEP 4 : Aesthetics Design
The aesthetic design of your GDD is a compilation of the visual and audial design of the game, using mood boards, concept art, sound effects, and music samples as a reference for what the game should look and sound like.

Deliverables for this step are:
- _Conceptual Moodboards_
- _Conceptual Soundscapes_
- _Prototypes of Art & Audio_

---

## Semantic Clarity & Consistency
Throughout all of your aesthetic design, you need to ensure that your aesthetic is clear and consistent. Your audience needs to be able to pick up on the information being conveyed to them by the visual and audial choices you make.

### _Establishing Meaning & Connection_
You can ensure clarity and consistency by establishing what certain visual and audial cues and motifs mean regarding the narrative and mechanics. This needs to be initialised so the player can register this association and remember it as they play.

## Visual Design

### Colour Theory & Psychology
Colour psychology is how people interpret and derive meaning from colours, and colour theory is the framework for how colours visually interact and cohere.

The way people interpret colour is based on biological, cultural, and personal factors. Biological factors tend to be universal, but cultural, personal, and contextual factors will vary per person and per situation. You may identify the cultural background of your audience for the sake of more accurate symbolism mapping.
