# P1-STEP 5 : Level Design
The level design of your GDD is an outline of how the game should be spaced, sectioned, and decorated according to the narrative implications, player dynamics, and aesthetic intentions.

Deliverables for this step are:
- _Beat Charts via Spreadsheet_
- _Level Profiles w/:_
    - _Intensity Level_
    - _Aesthetic Design_
    - _Narrative Details_
    - _Player Dynamics_
    - _Layout Diagrams_
